FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started with Katherine Career Services.

What information do I need to provide to get started?+
We typically need your current resume (if you have one), the job titles or roles you are targeting, and a brief overview of your career history and goals. You can send all of this directly to our email after reaching out through the contact form.
How long does the process take?+
Turnaround time depends on the package. Starter is typically 3 days, Professional is 48 hours, and Executive includes priority 24-hour delivery. Rush services may be available. Contact us to discuss your timeline.
Are your resumes guaranteed to get me interviews?+
While no service can guarantee interviews, our clients consistently report significantly higher callback rates after using our services. We stand firmly behind our work and offer revisions to ensure you are fully satisfied with the final result.
What industries do you specialize in?+
We work across a wide range of industries including technology, finance, healthcare, marketing, operations, education, legal, and more. Our writers research your specific field to ensure your documents reflect current industry expectations and standards.
Can I request changes after receiving my documents?+
Absolutely. All packages include at least one round of revisions, and higher-tier packages include multiple rounds or unlimited revisions. We work with you until you are completely happy with every detail of your document.
What is an executive bio and who needs one?+
An executive bio is a polished professional narrative used on company websites, conference programs, speaking engagements, board applications, and press materials. It is ideal for senior professionals, executives, entrepreneurs, and anyone who needs a compelling personal narrative beyond a traditional resume.
How do I pay and is my information secure?+
Payment details and secure methods will be discussed during your initial consultation. All client information and career documents are handled with complete confidentiality. We never share your data with third parties.
